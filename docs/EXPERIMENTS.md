## Ablations & Rejected Hypotheses

1. **`HYPERION_NOHUGE=1` (Disabling 2 MiB Transparent Huge Pages)**: Regressed `p20` wall time by `+1.0 s` (`3.63 s → 4.56 s`) due to 4 KB dTLB misses across scatter blocks and alias tables → **Rejected** (`MADV_HUGEPAGE` kept enabled with runtime `/sys/kernel/mm/transparent_hugepage/enabled` check).
2. **`HYPERION_IO=pread` vs `mmap`**: 512 KiB `pread` chunks regressed `p20` internal time by `+171 ms` (`2.0967 s` vs `1.9256 s`) and wall time by `+413 ms` on warm page cache due to extra kernel-to-user buffer copying → **Rejected** (kept zero-copy `mmap` + parallel 64 MiB `MADV_DONTNEED` PTE release before `_exit(0)`).
3. **Non-Temporal Streaming Stores (`_mm256_stream_si256` / `HYPERION_NT=1`)**: Regressed `parse+scatter` from `1,160 ms` to `2,617 ms` (`+1,457 ms`) because 2,048 concurrent partition write streams thrash the CPU's 12-entry write-combining (LFB) buffers → **Rejected** (kept 64B cache-line write-combining in L1/L2 staging buffers with write-back SIMD stores).
4. **Partition Count Sweep (`P × DS`)**: On 20M `p20`, `(P=512, DS=1024)` achieved `1.964 s` internal vs `(P=512, DS=512)` at `2.186 s`, `(P=256, DS=512)` at `2.398 s`, and `(P=256, DS=256)` at `2.495 s` → **Adopted `(512, 1024)` for 20M and `(1024, 2048)` for 60M**.
5. **Unquoted-Line Branch Fast Path (`if (dq == 0)`)**: Because the v5 generator quotes each field independently with probability `0.2`, $1 - 0.8^7 = 79.0\%$ of event lines contain at least one quoted field, causing severe branch mispredictions → **Rejected** (replaced with 100% branchless `base[p - isq]` quote stripping + `nlm` newline bitmask).
6. **Per-Thread Local Bloom Allocation (`lbloom_all`) in `links:pass3`**: Faulting 8 × 2 MiB THP regions + parallel OR merge added `+25 ms` overhead compared to direct `abloom.fetch_or` into the 1 MiB shared Bloom filter (`7.88 ms` on `p5`) → **Rejected**.
7. **Small-Dataset Synthetic Build-Time PGO**: Training PGO on a 400K-row (`< 16 MiB`) synthetic dataset trained the compiler on `P=64` and depth-1 DSU trees, marking `P=512/1024` and multi-hop DSU paths cold (`+30 ms` regression on `p5`) → **Rejected** in favor of explicit `__builtin_expect` annotations + GCC 14.2 `-O3`.
